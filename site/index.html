murad madi

These are the files that carry my name: the logos, the banners for each profile, the icons, the fonts and the email signature. Take what you need. Everything here also ships in the npm package, along with the theme my sites are built on.

Install

$ bun add @muradmadi/brand

@import "tailwindcss";
@import "@muradmadi/brand/fonts.css";
@import "@muradmadi/brand/theme.css";

Colours

Terminal is the default, paper is for a light preference and for print, and coral is the only accent in either. Click a colour to copy it.

Logos

Each on the ground it's made for. Keep one block's width clear around it, and don't set the mark smaller than 24 pixels. The PNGs are drawn at four times the SVG's size, for documents and slides.

Avatar

One square for every profile that doesn't get a photo. Platforms crop it to a circle, so the mark sits in the middle.

Banners

One per platform, at the size it asks for. The dashed boxes show what each platform keeps on every screen and where the profile photo covers it; they're not in the files.

Icons

The favicon, the home-screen icons and the link preview. At 16 pixels the block can't survive, so the smallest favicon is a coral tile with a dark M. A site serves the package's dist/icons/ at /icons/ and pastes head.html into its head.

Fonts

One face for the frame, one for the voice. Install these for documents, slides and print; sites load them from the package's fonts.css instead. Both are under the SIL Open Font License, which comes with each.

Email signature

Text only, so it looks the same in every mail app. To use it, copy it here and paste it into the signature box: in Gmail that's Settings, See all settings, General, Signature.

Profile

The facts the signature, the lockup and the banners are made from, in one file, so a site never has to repeat them.
